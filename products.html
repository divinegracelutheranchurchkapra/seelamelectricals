DWC Pipes & Ducts

Double walled corrugated (DWC) duct solutions. The supplied flyer lists advantages and standard sizes; confirm project specifications and availability with our team.

Enquire on WhatsApp →

Advantages listed in the flyer

  • Longer life compared with GI, RCC and PVC pipes.
  • Lightweight and easier to transport, handle and install.
  • Mechanical properties described as suitable for heavy loads.
  • Chemically inert, described as suitable for saline/coastal areas.
  • No sharp edges for duct/cable installation.
  • Flexible to take bends and curves around obstacles.
